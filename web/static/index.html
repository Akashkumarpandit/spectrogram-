System Architecture & Dataset Coverage

ASVspoof 2019 & 2021 Dataset Specs

  • Track LA (Logical Access): Speech synthesis (TTS: Tacotron, FastSpeech) and Voice Conversion (VCC) attacks.
  • Track DF (Deepfake): Lossy compressed speech, MP3/AAC/Opus codecs at variable bitrates.
  • Track PA (Physical Access): Acoustic room reverberation & microphone replay attacks.
  • Standard Metrics: Equal Error Rate (EER) and minimum tandem DCF (min t-DCF).

AASIST Model Pipeline

  • Front-End: Learnable SincNet band-pass filterbank directly on raw waveforms ($64,600$ samples).
  • 2D CNN Encoder: 4 Residual Convolutional blocks extracting spectro-temporal feature maps.
  • Graph Attention Network: Spectral and Temporal Graph Attention Layers with Graph Pooling.
  • Heterogeneous Fusion: Bidirectional cross-attention uniting time & frequency graphs into master embeddings.
  • Loss Criterion: Additive Margin Softmax (AM-Softmax, margin $m=0.2$, scale $s=30$).
